THE KFC STORY

From a small roadside restaurant to the world's most popular chicken chain - a journey of passion, quality, and finger lickin' good taste

OUR HERITAGE

It all started in 1930 when Colonel Harland Sanders created his unique blend of 11 herbs and spices in Corbin, Kentucky. What began as a roadside restaurant grew into a global phenomenon.

Today, KFC serves more than 12 million customers in over 145 countries and territories around the world. In India, we've been serving our signature chicken since 1995, bringing that authentic taste to millions of Indian families.

COLONEL SANDERS

Colonel Harland Sanders was an American businessman and entrepreneur who founded the Kentucky Fried Chicken restaurant chain. His journey from a struggling restaurateur to creating one of the world's most recognized brands is truly inspirational.

At the age of 65, after his restaurant was forced to close, the Colonel took his pressure cooker and his secret recipe on the road. He traveled across the country, cooking batches of his chicken for restaurant owners and their employees. If the reaction was favorable, he entered into a handshake agreement on a deal that stipulated a payment to him of a nickel for each chicken the restaurant sold.

"I made a resolve then that I was going to amount to something if I could. And no hours, nor amount of labor, nor amount of money would deter me from giving the best that there was in me."
- Colonel Harland Sanders

OUR VALUES

These core values guide everything we do, from how we prepare our food to how we serve our customers

Quality First

We never compromise on quality. Every piece of chicken is hand-breaded and made fresh throughout the day, following Colonel Sanders' original recipe with 11 herbs and spices.

Customer Focus

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. We strive to provide exceptional service and memorable dining experiences that keep them coming back.

Innovation

While we honor our traditions, we continuously innovate to meet changing tastes and preferences, introducing new products and improving our processes.

Sustainability

We are committed to sustainable practices, from responsible sourcing to reducing our environmental footprint and supporting local communities.

Team Spirit

We believe in the power of teamwork. Our success is built on the dedication and passion of our team members who work together to serve you better.

Giving Back

We actively contribute to the communities we serve through various charitable initiatives, education programs, and hunger relief efforts.

OUR QUALITY PROMISE

Every day, in every restaurant, we uphold the highest standards of food quality and safety

1

Fresh, Never Frozen

Our chicken is delivered fresh to our restaurants, never frozen. We hand-bread each piece throughout the day to ensure maximum freshness and flavor.

2

Original Recipe

Colonel Sanders' secret blend of 11 herbs and spices is still used today. The recipe is kept locked in a vault in Louisville, Kentucky.

3

Quality Ingredients

We source our chicken from trusted suppliers who meet our strict quality standards. Every ingredient is carefully selected and tested for quality.

Food Safety

Our restaurants follow rigorous food safety protocols. We maintain the highest hygiene standards and regularly train our team members.
